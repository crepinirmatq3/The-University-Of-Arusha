ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY OF ARUSHA

About the University of Arusha

The University of Arusha is a higher learning institution committed to providing quality education, research and innovation. The university aims to prepare students with knowledge, skills and values that will help them contribute positively to society.

OUR VISION

To become a leading university recognized for academic excellence and innovation.

OUR MISSION

To provide quality education and develop students with knowledge, skills and leadership qualities.

OUR VALUES

Excellence, integrity, innovation, respect and responsibility.
